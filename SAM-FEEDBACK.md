## Product model — packs, anchors, and desire

- **24 cards total, as 4 packs of 6.** (Reshapes the current 18-card set.)
- **Each pack is anchored by a lab leader** — the *headline trading card* that is the
  reason someone wants the pack. Packs are named for the leader: "Sam pack, Elon pack etc."
- **The technology / resource / chaos cards are "filler"** — *"important but not the reason
  someone will want the pack."* The headline card creates the pull.
- Reference dynamic: *"That was the dynamic at YC startup school when YC handed out cards."*
- *"Marketing is the art of creating desire."*
- **Named cards seeded into packs:** *"the OpenAI pack should have Tibo and Anthropic pack
  should have Boris."*

## Art direction — less robot, more human, more meme

- *"The human art is good."* — the human/character portraits (Garry Tan, Sam) land.
- *"Most of the cards are too robotic."* → *"way too many robots. no more than one robot
  anything per deck."* **Hard cap: ≤ 1 robot-themed card per pack.**
- *"If you're using a robot rethink the design."*
- Lean into **memes and human/character art** over generic robots. *"More memes in general."*
