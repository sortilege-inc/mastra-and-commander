THE COST CRESCENT — DETAIL

Four boundaries stack through the crescent: the plate’s double rule, two echo arcs offset outside the lens, and the lens rim itself — a three-line sandwich. The pip arc rides across all of them and the title plate drops a stacked edge over the top.

HOW IT’S BUILT
  • Pips trace the lens contour, swinging 66px between the first pip and the waist.
  • The frame runs under every plate; the art bleeds out of both right-hand corners.
  • Text box is 92% opaque, so art reads faintly through its lower band.
  • Each plate drops a solid stacked edge below-right — overlaps read as layered paper.
  • Multi-rules are stroke sandwiches on one path, so they are exactly concentric everywhere.
  • All four plates share one outer envelope, left 76 / right 748, inside the plate rule at 63.5 / 761.5.
LAYER INVENTORY — svg/v3/lens/

Each file is cropped to its own artwork. Place it at the listed origin on an 825×1125 canvas and the stack lines up. spec.json carries the same numbers machine-readably, plus the pip centres and the set-symbol position.
