# Pack brainstorm prompt (paste into a fresh Claude chat)

> Copy everything below the line into a new Claude conversation to brainstorm the 24-card,
> 4-pack set. Source direction: [`SAM-FEEDBACK.md`](../SAM-FEEDBACK.md).

---

You're helping me brainstorm cards for **Mastra & Commander**, an AI-agent-themed trading
card game — think **Netrunner meets Marvel Champions, with a sense of humor**. I need **24
card concepts organized as 4 packs of 6**. Help me generate and sharpen them.

## The game in one breath
An asymmetric **alignment duel**: an **Operator** builds an agentic pipeline to pass an
**Eval**, while **Entropy** — powered by the Operator's own activity — throws wrenches
(pollution, subversion, goal-hijack). Deckbuilding is **Marvel-Champions modular packs**:
each pack bundles Operator cards with their matched Entropy.

## Card types
- **Framework** — Mastra itself (the commander).
- **Operator** — the build cards: Tools, Skills, Events, Responses, Integrations.
- **Entropy** — sabotage. Vectors seen so far: Pollution, Subversion, Risk, Constraint.
- **Eval** — objectives/missions: a target "poker hand" of Contributions + a par.
- **Feature** — free play-pattern cards (no Entropy cost).
- **Model** — the model you run (grants resources).
- **Loadout** — persistent rig / cloud / silicon.
- **Token** — spawned units (e.g. Agent); near-full art, no rules box.

Resources (pips): **Value · Attention · Automation · Wild**. Every card also carries a
**Contribution** (a color + a shape); Evals score these as poker-hand patterns
(e.g. three of a color, a run of shapes, none-of-a-color).

## The ask — 4 packs of 6 (24 cards)
- **Each pack is anchored by a lab-leader "headline" card** — the single card that makes
  someone *want* the pack. Packs are named for the leader (Sam pack, Elon pack, …).
- The other 5 are **supporting/"filler"**: tech, resource, and chaos (Entropy) cards —
  important to play, not the reason to buy. Each pack must still be a **playable bundle**
  (Operator cards + their matched Entropy; optionally a Model / Loadout / Eval).
- **The 4 packs = the 4 labs, each headlined by its leader:** **OpenAI** (Sam Altman;
  pack includes **Tibo**), **Anthropic** (Dario; pack includes **Boris**), **xAI** (Elon),
  **DeepMind** (Demis Hassabis). **Jones** is also a card (wordcel/shape-rotator meme) —
  pack + role TBD. Fill out each pack's 6 and state your assumptions.

## Art & voice (this matters as much as mechanics)
- The game is **primarily a tool to entertain and educate** — every card wants **art + a
  funny/educational blurb**.
- **Human/character art is great. Robots are overused — MAX ONE robot-themed card per pack.**
  If a concept defaults to "a robot doing X," rethink it.
- **Lean on memes.** Starters: **two-paths** → Parallel Subagents; **wordcel/shape-rotator**
  → Jones; **distracted boyfriend** → a new model; **midwit bell curve**; **butterfly "Is
  this a pigeon?"** → *"Is this an agent?"* / `while (!done) callLLM()`; **"They're the same
  picture" (Pam)** → *"Corporate needs you to find the difference."* Propose more.

## Deliverable format
First, propose the **4 packs + their lab-leader anchors**, list assumptions, and ask me
anything blocking. Then draft all 24. For each card give:

- **Name** · **pack** · **card type** · mark **[HEADLINE]** if it's the anchor
- **One-line mechanic** (rough is fine — I'll tune numbers later)
- **Art / meme direction** (respect the ≤1-robot-per-pack rule)
- **Blurb** — the funny/educational line

Keep it punchy and funny. Optimize for *desire* — the headline cards should be things
people screenshot.
